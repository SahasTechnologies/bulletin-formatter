Project proposal

Tropic · Summer sustainability program

Goal

Reduce single-use plastics across the school grounds by installing three refill stations before the end of the term.

Approach

  1. Survey current water-cooler usage and waste bins.
  2. Source reusable bottles and signage for each station.
  3. Launch with a student-led awareness week.

Budget

Estimated $1,240, funded by the SRC green grant.
