The power of small habits

An essay about how tiny choices compound

Everyone dreams of big changes, but they rarely arrive in a single event. Instead, progress tends to come from the quiet accumulation of daily decisions that, on their own, hardly seem to matter.

A habit is a choice repeated so often it becomes automatic. By removing the need to decide, habits keep us moving on the days when motivation is scarce, which is exactly when discipline matters most.

Of course, not every habit is worth keeping. The value of a routine is not in its size but in its direction. One percent a day might feel slow, yet it compounds into something remarkable over a year.
